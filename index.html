Process

How a job gets done right.

You deal with Phil from the first site visit to the final walk-through. No sales team, no crew of strangers sent in his place, and it is not done until it is done right.

  1. 1

    Site visit

    Phil comes out, looks at what you have and what you have been wanting, measures, and tells you straight what it will take. A photo or two is enough to book it.

  2. 2

    Written quote

    Phil works up the layout, materials and finish with you and gives you a clear written quote and timeline before anything is ordered or cut.

  3. 3

    The build

    Decks, pergolas, remodels and built-ins are built on site, with the job kept clean and moving. Furniture is built in the shop on the ranch. Either way you see progress as it happens.

  4. 4

    Final walk-through

    Phil walks you through the finished work, covers how to care for it, and does not call it done until you do.

Phil Gielow

Three generations on the same ground.

Phil grew up in Gardnerville, Nevada, on the ranch where PG Customs is based today. His grandfather was a woodworker. His father could build just about anything. Phil has been building since he was a kid, and every job he takes on carries all three of them.

He runs the company himself, is on site for every job, and keeps you in the loop from the first visit to the final walk-through. If it is on your list, he can build it, rebuild it or fix it. The work ranges from decks, pergolas, remodels and additions to the headboards and Viking-inspired tables that go inside, built from lumber sourced around the Tahoe area and made to last.
