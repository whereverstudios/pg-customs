Process

How a piece gets made.

You deal with Phil from the first conversation to the day it is installed. No showroom, no sales team.

  1. 1

    The conversation

    Tell Phil about the room, how the piece will be used and what you like. Rough dimensions and a photo or two are plenty.

  2. 2

    Design and quote

    Phil works up the design, wood and finish with you and gives you a clear quote before anything is cut.

  3. 3

    The build

    Built by hand in the shop on the ranch in Gardnerville, from lumber sourced around the Tahoe area. You see progress along the way.

  4. 4

    Delivery and install

    Phil delivers and sets the piece himself, or builds it in place for decks, pergolas and additions.

Phil Gielow

Three generations on the same ground.

Phil grew up in Gardnerville, Nevada, on the ranch where PG Customs builds today. His grandfather was a woodworker. His father could build just about anything. Phil has been in the shop since he was a kid, and every piece that leaves it carries all three of them.

He builds with lumber sourced around the Tahoe area, assembles everything by hand and keeps you in the loop from the first sketch to the day it is set in your home. The work ranges from headboards and Viking-inspired tables to decks and pergolas, and all of it is made to last a lifetime.
